FAQ

How fast do alerts arrive?

jobhunt checks every tracked company about every 30 minutes, day and night. Most alerts arrive within an hour of a role going up.

Why didn't I get an alert for a role?

  • The company isn't tracked. Check the companies page.
  • It's outside your filters: country, level or graduation date.
  • The title isn't software engineering or close to it.
  • It went up before you subscribed, or more than about a day and a half before jobhunt saw it.

Two alerts have the same title. Is that a repeat?

No. A different Job ID means a separate opening, often in a different city or team.

How do I change my settings or stop alerts?

Open the manage link from the confirmation message in your Discord channel. Lost it? Enter the same webhook on the home page and a new link is posted in your channel. Your settings stay the same.

Is it free? What do you store?

It's free. jobhunt stores your webhook URL (encrypted) and your preferences. See the privacy page.

Can you add a company?

Yes. Tell me through the feedback form.
